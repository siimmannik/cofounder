COFOUNDER — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

COFOUNDER ("the game") is a satirical single-player game published by Alt-text Games, a trading name of Alltekst OÜ (Estonia).

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Local storage

Your game progress (runs played, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data deletes it permanently.

Advertising

The game currently shows fictional, satirical ads that are part of the game's content. They are not real advertisements, are not targeted, and do not track you. If real advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
